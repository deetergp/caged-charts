CAGED Scale Positions

Five positions each for the major and minor scales, built on the CAGED chord shapes. Pick a key and the whole sheet moves with it.

Dot labels
Root Chord tone (3rd, 5th) Other scale tone High e on top, as in tab. Numbers under each chart are fret numbers.
Tip: a minor key and its relative major share the same five shapes. A minor uses exactly the C major patterns, just with a different note treated as home. The minor shapes are named after the minor chord they surround (Em, Dm, Cm, Am, Gm), and Box 1 is the familiar pentatonic box with the root under your first finger on the low E string.
